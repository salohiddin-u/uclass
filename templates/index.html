TRUST STARTS WITH UNDERSTANDING

Your academic data deserves transparency.

We want you to understand what information is collected, why it is needed, and how you can control it.

Read the Privacy Policy
Encrypted credential storage

To synchronize information that requires a LMS web login, uclass.uz stores an encrypted copy of your LMS password, only with your explicit consent.

Encryption reduces risk but does not eliminate it. The server must decrypt credentials to establish authorized LMS sessions. This is not end-to-end encryption.

Permission-based access

Academic information is accessed through the LMS account you choose to connect, subject to the university’s permissions.

Only supported, authorized sources are used.

You control your data

You can disconnect your account and request deletion of your stored credentials and synchronized data.

An independent student project

uclass.uz is an independent student project and is not an official service of Inha University or LMS.

No university endorsement, security certification, audit, or partnership is implied.

A FEW THINGS, EXPLAINED

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Know what you’re connecting to
and how your data is handled.

Connect your account
Is uclass.uz an official university platform?

No. uclass.uz is an independent student project designed to make academic information easier to understand.

Where does my academic information come from?

Supported information is retrieved from the university’s LMS platform using authorized access associated with the account you connect.

Can I see attendance for individual classes?

Yes. uclass.uz shows session-by-session attendance, including dates, statuses, and points where available.

Why does uclass.uz need my LMS password?

Some detailed attendance information is available through authenticated LMS web pages rather than the student REST API. With your explicit permission, encrypted credential storage enables automatic synchronization of those records.

Encryption reduces risk, but storing credentials is not risk-free.

Can I delete my information?

Yes. You can disconnect your account and request deletion of your stored credentials and synchronized academic data. See the Privacy Policy for details and limitations.
