Good questions. Clear answers.

Is uclass.uz an official university platform?

No. uclass.uz is an independent student project designed to make academic information easier to understand.

Where does my academic information come from?

Supported information comes from your authorized university Moodle account. Availability depends on the records and permissions provided by the university.

Can I see attendance for individual classes?

Attendance records can include dates, statuses, and points where those details are provided. Unrecorded attendance is not automatically considered absent.

Why might uclass.uz need my Moodle password?

Some detailed records require an authenticated Moodle web session. Review the credential handling information and Privacy Policy before connecting your account.

Can I delete my information?

The Privacy Policy describes data retention and the available deletion process. Account controls are available from your profile when supported.
