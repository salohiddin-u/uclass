GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
made clear.

A clearer workspace starts
with knowing what to expect.

Do I need to sign in?

Yes. Sign in to open your personal workspace, where you can see the courses and academic records supplied to your account.

What if a grade or due date is missing?

ihawk shows the records that are available. Unrecorded attendance is not automatically absent, and “Not graded” does not mean overdue or incomplete.

Can I use ihawk on my phone?

Yes. The workspace adapts to your screen, with bottom navigation on phones and a sidebar on larger screens.

Can I export my records?

You can export recorded attendance and course grades as CSV from the relevant page.
