How we protect your LMS credentials

To keep your courses, attendance, grades, and assignments up to date automatically, uclass.uz securely stores an encrypted copy of your LMS password on the server.

  • The password is encrypted before being stored in the database.
  • The password is used to establish authorized LMS sessions and synchronize academic information.
  • Encryption does not make storing passwords risk-free.
  • uclass.uz is a third-party service, not the official LMS or University website.
  • You may disconnect your LMS account and request deletion of stored credentials and synchronized personal data.
